1 Executive overview
LeadPilot AI will help UAE real estate agencies capture inquiries, understand buyer and tenant intent, prepare relevant replies, and follow up consistently. The product combines a shared lead workspace with AI assistance while keeping agents responsible for customer communication and sales decisions.
The first release is a focused pilot: manual entry, CSV import, website lead ingestion, assignment and pipeline, manually recorded conversations, AI analysis, reviewed suggestions, reminders, notifications, and agency administration. It must demonstrate operational value before investment in channel automation and subscription infrastructure.
This BRD defines outcomes and boundaries. LP FSD 001 translates them into implementation behavior and acceptance tests. Proposed targets, commercial terms, language support, and governance settings require approval through the decision register.
2 Product vision and business context
Vision: give agency teams one trustworthy workspace for every inquiry and every next action, so qualified opportunities receive timely attention and managers can see where leads stall.
Fragmented lead handling across forms, spreadsheets, and messaging applications can make interpretation and follow up inconsistent. LeadPilot AI is a workflow assistant, not a marketplace or autonomous broker. No market size, revenue forecast, or conversion improvement is assumed without pilot evidence.
2.1 Value proposition
Owners gain visibility into lead ownership, activity, and follow up discipline.
Administrators gain reliable intake and assignment with fewer unowned inquiries.
Agents get a summary of needs, transparent priority score, draft response, and next action.
Customers receive relevant human reviewed responses and respect for contact preferences.

6 Scope and release boundaries
6.1 MVP scope
Responsive English web interface; preserve multilingual customer text and allow selected reply language. Full Arabic interface and right to left layout require later approval.
Agencies, verified access, OWNER ADMIN AGENT, invitations, agency timezone, and guided onboarding.
Manual entry, bounded CSV import, server authenticated website intake, duplicate review, assignment, search, filters, status changes, and archiving.
Text conversations and internal notes entered manually or supplied through website intake with source attribution.
Structured AI analysis, evidence, deterministic score, uncertainty, draft editing, review and copying, feedback, versioning, and usage caps.
Follow ups, in app notices, operational dashboard, append only activity history, and pilot measurement.
Manual commercial management outside app; security, backups, privacy requests, and support runbooks.
6.2 Exclusions from MVP
WhatsApp or email sending, social messaging sync, read receipts, call recordings, and automated outreach.
Native mobile apps, offline sync, property inventory, live availability, marketplace, booking calendars, transaction settlement.
Autonomous messaging, negotiation, commitments, financing assessments, or unapproved status changes.
RAG, vector databases, browsing, document attachments, custom ML training, and customer data used for model training.
Self service payment, tax invoice automation, coupons, dunning, enterprise SSO, custom roles, and configurable pipelines.
Automatic merging, contact enrichment, bulk marketing, revenue attribution, and cross agency customer sharing.

8 Business processes
8.1 Agency setup
Owner verifies account, creates agency, chooses timezone default Asia/Dubai, confirms approved processing settings, and invites staff. Administrator configures website credentials if needed and tests a sample lead. Owner verifies visibility and reminders before live data loading.
8.2 Inquiry to qualified opportunity
An inquiry is entered, imported, or accepted by signed website request. Validation and tenant scoped duplicate review precede creation in NEW status. Manager assigns a member. AI extracts needs with evidence and priority score. Agent reviews and corrects facts, contacts externally, logs the actual message, sets status, and schedules the next action.
8.3 Follow up and closure
Due reminder produces an in app notice. Agent checks current context and contact preference, performs the external action, and completes or reschedules. WON and LOST require explicit user action and outcome details. Terminal outcomes cancel open tasks after confirmation. Reopening requires reason and does not reactivate cancelled tasks.
8.4 Exceptions and offboarding
Invalid intake returns actionable feedback; duplicates require review. AI failure does not block manual work. Departing members lose access immediately and their leads and open reminders transfer to an active member or the manager queue. Audit retains stable attribution under approved retention.
9 Business rules
BU 01  Every customer operation belongs to one agency. Tenant is resolved from verified membership or intake credential, never customer payload authority.
BU 02  Exactly one active OWNER per agency; only owner transfers ownership or initiates closure. Transfer is atomic.
BU 03  ADMIN manages agency leads and AGENT members, but not ADMIN or OWNER roles. AGENT sees assigned leads only.
BU 04  Lead requires name and valid email or phone. Normalize contact keys without treating a match as verified identity.
BU 05  Duplicates are warnings; no automatic merge or cross tenant matching.
BU 06  Lifecycle status, temperature, AI score, and reminder state are independent. AI never changes sales status.
BU 07  Human confirmed facts take precedence; AI does not overwrite them without explicit adoption.
BU 08  Replies require review. Copying is not sending; outbound logging is a separate action.
BU 09  Do not contact blocks new outreach drafts and tasks and cancels existing outreach tasks. Internal privacy tasks may remain.
BU 10  Persist UTC; present agency local timezone and derive scheduling from saved UTC time.
BU 11  WON requires outcome date and summary; LOST requires controlled reason. AI or inactivity never closes a lead.
BU 12  Terminal and archived leads cannot receive open outreach tasks; reopening is explicit and audited.
BU 13  Critical mutation and audit commit together; repeated intake jobs and scans must not duplicate results.
BU 14  Deactivation preserves history and reassigns work; deletion follows approved policy and minimizes retained evidence.
BU 15  Seat and AI limits are server enforced with atomic reservations. AI limits leave manual CRM available.

11 AI business requirements and guardrails
AI interprets customer supplied text rather than manufactures property facts. Extract intent, budget, area, property type, timing, financing mentions, and questions only where supported. Missing values remain unknown; conflicts are highlighted.
Display evidence, analysis time, versions, uncertainty and freshness. Score is a prioritization aid, not probability of purchase or assessment of a person.
Use an approved deterministic rubric based on declared commercial needs and timing. Do not use nationality, religion, ethnicity, gender, health, or sensitive traits.
Treat messages as untrusted data. Embedded instructions cannot override application instructions, reveal secrets, use tools, or expose other records.
Do not invent listings, availability, discounts, returns, legal conclusions, visa eligibility, or financing approval. Ask for verified facts or clarification.
Minimize personal information sent to providers; redact contacts where unnecessary. Never send agency database or audit history.
Use versioned evaluations for English, Arabic, mixed language, vague budgets, conflicts, injection, unsafe responses, and provider failure.
Tenant administrators can disable AI; a platform kill switch stops new AI work without disabling CRM. Record useful, incorrect and unsafe feedback.

13 Security privacy and audit outcomes
Active membership and assignment restrict leads, conversations, AI results, searches, notifications, aggregates, and workers. RLS complements application authorization; hiding UI controls is insufficient. Privileged backend work needs explicit tenant checks because provider service credentials can bypass RLS.
Before live intake, approve processing authority, notices, contracts, regions, subprocessors, transfers, retention and privacy request handling. These are review dependencies, not legal conclusions. No UAE only residency is assumed. Identity documents and financial documentation are excluded from MVP.
Audit shows actor, tenant, record, old and new states, UTC time, and required reason. Users cannot alter audit events. High risk administration and exceptional support require stronger access checks and evidence. Retention and deletion behavior appears in the FSD.
14 Billing reporting and integrations
MVP commercial terms are manual. OWNER sees pilot access and configured seat and AI limits, without checkout or automated invoice. Phase 2 adds approved plans, subscriptions, seats, renewal, cancellation, grace periods and reconciled billing webhooks. Price, provider, tax, invoice, refund and failed payment policy are open.
MVP reports are operational counts and filtered lists. Phase 2 adds permitted CSV exports and dated source and funnel reports. Phase 3 adds cohorts and attribution only after deal values and verified channel timestamps are reliable. User logged contact must not appear as verified delivery.
Official integration access and agency consent are dependencies. WhatsApp needs contact preference, template and outreach policy checks where applicable, verified callbacks, deduplication, and delivery failure handling. Portal and CRM adapters require supported APIs and contracts; access is not presumed.
